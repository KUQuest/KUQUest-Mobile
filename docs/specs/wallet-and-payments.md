# Wallet and Payments

## Status

Deferred for a later domain-design discussion.

## Known direction

KU Account Holders have access to top-up and withdrawal capabilities. A Hirer funds a Quest, and Quest rewards are defined per Worker. Quest funding, reward settlement, and withdrawal behavior must be resolved before implementation.

The current Quest model assumes that the per-Worker reward is reserved before a Quest becomes discoverable and that unused reserved funds return to the Hirer when places remain unfilled or a Quest is cancelled, except for rewards already earned by completed Workers.

## Questions for the later discussion

- Does every KU Account Holder have a separate available balance?
- Who may top up and withdraw?
- When are funds reserved, released, and credited to a Worker?
- What are the withdrawal methods, minimums, fees, pending states, and failure/refund rules?
